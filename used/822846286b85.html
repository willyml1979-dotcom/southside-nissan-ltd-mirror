Enjoy premium electric driving with this EQB300 4MATIC SUV, combining responsive all-electric performance, confident all-wheel-drive traction, and versatile everyday practicality. Its dual-motor electric powertrain delivers smooth, quiet acceleration, while the spacious cabin and flexible seating make it ideal for daily commuting and family adventures.

The Exclusive Trim adds premium convenience and technology, including KEYLESS-GO, a foot-activated tailgate, an advanced sound system, wireless phone charging, and augmented-reality navigation. The AMG Line brings sporty exterior styling, sport seats, a sport steering wheel, stainless-steel pedals, and AMG floor mats.

The Parking Package with 360° Camera provides a birds-eye view to help with tight parking manoeuvres. Additional highlights include an illuminated Mercedes-Benz star, adjustable second-row seating for added passenger and cargo flexibility, 18-inch AMG 10-spoke wheels, all-season tires, black fabric roof liner, and black open-pore linden wood trim.
