The 2025 Honda Odyssey Black Edition makes busy family life feel a little more luxurious. Combining bold, exclusive Black Edition styling with exceptional comfort, innovative technology and outstanding versatility, this premium minivan is designed to make every journey more enjoyable.

With its spacious three-row cabin, flexible seating arrangements and generous cargo capacity, the Odyssey offers plenty of room for family, friends, sports equipment and everything that comes along for the ride.

Powered by a 3.5L V6 engine producing 280 horsepower and 262 lb-ft of torque, paired with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission, the Odyssey delivers refined performance, responsive acceleration and a comfortable driving experience.
