2017 Nissan Pathfinder S — Family-Friendly Space With V6 Performance

The 2017 Nissan Pathfinder S combines strong V6 performance, everyday comfort and impressive versatility in a spacious three-row SUV. Its 3.5L direct-injected V6 engine produces 284 horsepower and 259 lb-ft of torque, paired with Nissan's Xtronic continuously variable transmission for smooth acceleration and confident highway driving.

For growing families, daily commuters and drivers who need additional passenger space, the Pathfinder S delivers a practical combination of comfort, capability and convenience. With seating for seven passengers, flexible second- and third-row seating, tri-zone automatic climate control and an eight-inch touchscreen display, the Pathfinder is designed to accommodate busy family schedules, road trips and everyday adventures.

The innovative EZ Flex seating system makes accessing the third row easier, while the spacious cargo area provides room for groceries, sports equipment, luggage and other everyday essentials. With available four-wheel drive and towing capability when properly equipped, the Pathfinder offers additional versatility for changing driving conditions and weekend travel.
