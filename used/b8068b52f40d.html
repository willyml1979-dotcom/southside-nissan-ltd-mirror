6.2L V8 | 420 HP | 4X4 | AT4 OFF-ROAD PACKAGE | LEATHER INTERIOR | HEATED & VENTILATED FRONT SEATS | ONYX BLACK

Built for capability, comfort, and adventure, this GMC Sierra 1500 AT4 Crew Cab combines powerful V8 performance with rugged off-road styling. Its 6.2L EcoTec3 V8 delivers 420 horsepower and 460 lb-ft of torque, paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission and four-wheel drive for confident performance on the road and off the beaten path.

The AT4 package adds a factory 2-inch suspension lift, off-road suspension with monotube shocks, a two-speed transfer case, an automatic locking rear differential, and underbody skid plates. Inside, enjoy a premium Jet Black leather interior with Kalahari accents, perforated leather seating, and a refined cabin designed for comfort on long drives.

Finished in striking Onyx Black, this Sierra features perforated leather upholstery with Kalahari accents, combining a bold exterior with a distinctive interior. The spacious Crew Cab provides generous room for passengers, while the short box offers practical cargo versatility for work and weekend activities.

Package & Feature Highlights:

AT4 Off-Road Package: Factory 2-inch lift, off-road suspension, skid plates, and two-speed transfer case.

6.2L V8 Engine: 420 hp and 460 lb-ft of torque for strong performance.

Premium Leather Interior: Jet Black perforated leather with Kalahari accents.

Four-Wheel Drive: Added traction for challenging road and trail conditions.

Onyx Black Exterior: Bold, premium finish.
