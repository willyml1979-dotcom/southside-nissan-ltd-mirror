Discover the perfect balance of British luxury, dynamic performance, and all-terrain capability in this2023 Land Rover Discovery P360 R-Dynamic HSE. Under the hood, the Discovery is powered by a 3.0L turbocharged inline-six engine featuring mild-hybrid technology, producing an impressive355 horsepower and 369 lb-ft of torque. Paired with a smooth 8-speed automatic transmission and intelligent all-wheel drive, it delivers responsive acceleration, refined highway cruising, and confident performance in a variety of driving conditions.

TheR-Dynamic HSEtrim adds a sportier and more distinctive appearance with unique R-Dynamic styling details, premium exterior accents, and an upscale interior. With its spacious three-row seating configuration, generous passenger room, and flexible cargo space, the Discovery is exceptionally well suited for family life and longer road trips.

Whether navigating city streets, tackling challenging weather, or heading out on your next adventure, the Discovery delivers confidence and comfort every step of the way.
