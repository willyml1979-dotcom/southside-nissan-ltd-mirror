2.9L TWIN-TURBO V6 | 375 HP | PDK TRANSMISSION | ALL-WHEEL DRIVE | SPORTY LUXURY SUV | METALLIC PAINT

Experience Porsche performance and everyday versatility in this Macan S. Its 2.9L twin-turbocharged V6 produces 375 horsepower and 383 lb-ft of torque, paired with Porsche’s 7-speed PDK dual-clutch transmission and all-wheel drive for rapid acceleration, precise handling, and confident traction. The sport-tuned chassis delivers an engaging driving experience while maintaining the comfort and practicality expected from a luxury SUV.

The Macan S features distinctive Porsche styling, muscular body lines, a refined cabin, and a driver-focused cockpit. Its updated interior includes a 10.9-inch touchscreen infotainment system, modern touch-sensitive centre console controls, and premium materials throughout. With flexible cargo space and comfortable seating, it is equally suited to daily commuting and weekend getaways.

Finished in metallic paint, this Macan S combines an elegant exterior appearance with the performance-focused character that defines Porsche.
