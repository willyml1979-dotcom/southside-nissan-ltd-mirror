The 2027 Nissan Murano Dark Armor AWD pairs a 2.0L Variable Compression Turbo engine with a 9-speed automatic transmission and Intelligent All-Wheel Drive. Its distinctive exterior includes 21-inch wheels with Dark Armor caps, sport-type front and rear bumpers, unique side and window mouldings, gloss-black accents and Dark Armor badging.

Inside, leatherette-appointed seating, heated front and rear seats, a heated steering wheel and a panoramic moonroof provide year-round comfort. Dual 12.3-inch displays, Google built-in, Bose 10-speaker audio and wireless smartphone connectivity add useful technology, while ProPILOT Assist and the 3D Intelligent Around View Monitor support everyday driving.
