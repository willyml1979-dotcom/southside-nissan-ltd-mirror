Twin-Turbo V6 | 20" PRO-4X Wheels | Skid Plate | 3D Cam | Dual 14.3" Displays | Heated & Cooled Front Seats | Heated Rear | Klipsch Audio | Pano Moonroof

The 2027 Nissan Armada PRO-4X 4WD pairs an electronic locking rear differential and adaptive electronic air suspension with all-terrain tires on 20-inch PRO-4X alloy wheels. Its 3.5L twin-turbo V6 produces 425 hp and 516 lb-ft of torque on premium fuel, paired with a 9-speed automatic transmission. A metal underbody skid plate, Lava Red tow hooks and an off-road front bumper with an increased approach angle distinguish its capability-focused equipment.

Inside, eight-passenger seating, PRO-4X TailorFit seating surfaces and heated and cooled front seats combine utility with comfort. Heated second-row seats, a dual panoramic moonroof and Klipsch 12-speaker audio complement the 14.3-inch touchscreen and 14.3-inch driver display. A fully rotating 3D Around View Monitor with Invisible Hood View assists with manoeuvring, while an integrated trailer brake controller adds towing convenience.
