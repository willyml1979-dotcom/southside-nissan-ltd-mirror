The 2027 Nissan ARIYA Platinum+ e-4ORCE is an all-electric SUV with dual-motor all-wheel drive, an 87 kWh battery and 394 km of range. Nappa leather seating, heated and cooled front seats, and a power panoramic moonroof make the cabin comfortable year-round.

The head-up display projects driving information onto the windshield, and the 3D Around View Monitor helps you see around the vehicle when parking. The motion-activated liftgate is handy when your hands are full, and the 10-speaker Bose system handles audio.
