The 2027 Nissan Kicks SR Premium AWD combines Bose 10-speaker audio with driver and front passenger headrest speakers, a panoramic moonroof and 19-inch alloy wheels. A 2.0L four-cylinder engine and Xtronic CVT pair with all-wheel drive and Snow Mode. ProPILOT Assist and an HD Intelligent Around View Monitor with Moving Object Detection and enhanced 2D views expand the driver-assistance equipment.

The 12.3-inch touchscreen includes wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, with wireless phone charging and front and rear USB-C ports for compatible devices. Heated front seats, a heated steering wheel and remote engine start add everyday comfort.
