The 2027 Nissan LEAF SV+ FWD pairs a 75-kWh battery with a 463-km range, an 11-kW onboard AC charger and NACS DC fast charging up to 150 kW. Regenerative braking paddles add convenient control, while 18-inch alloy wheels distinguish the exterior. TailorFit front seats, leatherette rear seats and an 8-way power driver's seat upgrade the cabin.

Heated front and rear seats, a heated steering wheel and wireless phone charging add everyday comfort and convenience. The 3D Around View Monitor with fixed views and Moving Object Detection helps when manoeuvring, alongside ProPILOT Assist. Dual 12.3-inch displays, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, and six-speaker audio complete the technology, with Divide-N-Hide storage helping organise the cargo area.
