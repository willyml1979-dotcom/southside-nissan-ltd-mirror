The 2026 Nissan Rogue Platinum AWD combines quilted, perforated semi-aniline leather-appointed seats with Bose 10-speaker premium audio and a Head-Up Display. A 12.3-inch HD touchscreen and 12.3-inch digital dashboard bring larger displays to the cabin, alongside wireless Apple CarPlay, wireless Android Auto and a wireless charging pad. Its 201-hp 1.5L turbocharged engine is paired with an Xtronic CVT and Intelligent All-Wheel Drive.

An enhanced HD Intelligent Around View Monitor with eight fixed 3D views, front and rear parking sensors and ProPILOT Assist expand the driver-assistance equipment. A panoramic moonroof, heated front and rear seats, tri-zone automatic climate control and driver's seat memory add comfort. The motion-activated power liftgate and Divide-N-Hide cargo system simplify loading and organising everyday essentials.
