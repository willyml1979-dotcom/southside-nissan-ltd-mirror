14.3" Touchscreen | Touchless Liftgate | Bose 10-Speaker Audio | Head-Up Display | 3D Around View | Colour Ambient Lighting | 19" Alloy Wheels | 417km Range

The 2027 Nissan LEAF Platinum+ FWD combines a 75-kWh battery and 417-km range with a dimming panoramic roof, Bose Personal Plus 10-speaker audio and a Head-Up Display. A 14.3-inch touchscreen and 14.3-inch digital driver display bring larger screens to the cabin, while NACS DC fast charging up to 150 kW and an 11-kW onboard AC charger support charging on the road and at home.

TailorFit front seats, leatherette rear seats and heated seating in both rows provide comfort, with an 8-way power driver's seat, 4-way power lumbar adjustment and memory settings. A motion-activated power liftgate and Divide-N-Hide storage simplify cargo access. The 3D Around View Monitor, ProPILOT Assist, wireless phone charging and multicolour ambient lighting complete the flagship trim.
