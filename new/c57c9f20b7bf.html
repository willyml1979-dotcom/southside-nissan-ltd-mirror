The 2027 Nissan LEAF S FWD combines a 53-kWh battery and 174-hp electric motor with a 341-km range. A NACS charging port supports DC fast charging up to 105 kW, while a 7.2-kW onboard charger handles AC charging. A heat pump, battery heater and heated front seats provide useful equipment for Canadian driving.

Inside, a 12.3-inch touchscreen with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto sits alongside a 12.3-inch digital driver display. ProPILOT Assist, Intelligent Blind Spot Intervention and Rear Cross Traffic Alert add driver assistance, while a rearview camera and front and rear parking sensors help when manoeuvring. Zero Gravity front and rear outboard seats and a 60/40 split-folding rear seat combine passenger comfort with cargo flexibility.
